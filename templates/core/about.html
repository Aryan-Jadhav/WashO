About WashO

Laundry made simple, and honest about every single garment.

Why we started

Online laundry saves people hours every week. But the most common complaint about these services is simple: "Some of my clothes didn't come back." Once a bag of clothes leaves the house, customers have no way of knowing what happened to each item.

WashO fixes this by giving every garment its own QR tag at the store. We count your clothes three times: when our agent picks them up, when they reach the store, and when they come back to you. If any count doesn't match, the system alerts our admin team immediately, long before you notice.

What we believe

  • Transparency: clear per-item prices and a live order status.
  • Care: stains and damage are noted (with a photo) before cleaning, not argued about afterwards.
  • Convenience: pickup slots that fit your day, and online payment or cash on delivery.
